FlexiCubes-Based Polygonization of Geometric Models Defined by R-Functions

Lisniak Andrii[0000-0001-9669-7858],

Roman Kotov[0009-0008-5283-2972],

Serhii Choporov [0000-0001-5932-952X]

Abstract R-functions provide a powerful analytic framework for constructing complex geometric domains through continuous Boolean composition of implicit primitives. Despite their strong mathematical foundation and precise boundary control, generating high-quality polygonal approximations of R-function-defined objects remains challenging, particularly in the presence of sharp features and complex topology.

In this paper, we investigate the application of the FlexiCubes isosurface extraction method to polygonize geometric objects defined via R-functions. Unlike classical Marching Cubes and Dual Contouring algorithms, FlexiCubes employs a flexible dual isosurface formulation with additional degrees of freedom for vertex positioning and element splitting control, enabling improved feature preservation and enhanced mesh quality.

We perform a comparative evaluation on a set of analytically constructed models involving Boolean compositions and geometric singularities. The resulting meshes are assessed using geometric fidelity, topological correctness, and element quality metrics. Experimental results demonstrate a reduction in sliver triangles, improved sharp feature reconstruction, and increased mesh regularity compared to traditional spatial decomposition approaches.

The proposed methodology establishes an effective bridge between analytic R-function-based modeling and modern high-quality isosurface extraction techniques.

Keywords: R-functions, implicit surface modeling, isosurface extraction, FlexiCubes, high-quality mesh generation, polygonization, geometric modeling
